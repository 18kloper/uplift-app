How Matching Works

How we match founders & mentors.

Every founder gets paired with a mentor based on real signal from both applications, not a coin flip and not alphabetical order. Here’s what actually goes into it.

1

We start with what you told us

Your application: where your company is today, what you need from a mentor, and your availability.

2

We compare that to every mentor’s profile

Their focus areas, what they’re offering, and their availability. We look for real overlap.

3

Our tool surfaces the strongest candidates

Not one "correct" answer, a ranked shortlist of mentors who align well with you.

4

A person makes the final call

Every match is confirmed by our team, never assigned automatically.

What actually factors in

Time commitment fit

Your expected meeting cadence matched against what your mentor has signed up to give.

Focus area alignment

Topics you want help with (fundraising, GTM, hiring, product) against what your mentor knows best.

What you're hoping for, in your own words

Real overlap between what you said you need and what your mentor offers.

Stage fit

Where your company is now against the stage your mentor usually works with.

Schedule overlap

Whether your general availability (mornings, evenings, weekends, or flexible) has a real window in common.

How the scoring works

  • Shared language · your words vs. their offer+1 to +2
  • Stage fit+2
  • Schedule overlap+1

The short version

Matching starts with real data from both sides, gets narrowed by a tool built specifically for this program, and always ends with a human decision. If something about your match doesn’t feel right, tell your Program Manager. That’s exactly the kind of signal the process is built to respond to.

Can I request a specific mentor?

Reach out to your Program Manager. We can’t promise a specific pairing, but we do take requests into account.

What if the match isn’t working?

Tell us. Rematching is a normal, judgment-free part of the process, not a last resort.

Questions? uplift@techunited.co, or ask your Program Manager directly.
