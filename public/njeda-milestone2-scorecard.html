NJEDA · Milestone 2 ($20k/cohort)
July 14, 2026
Grant Requirement — Milestone 2 (Cohort Completion) · $20,000 per cohort, max $160,000
Each Cohort must have: “A minimum of ten (10) Cohort participants that are New Jersey residents and new to the Program complete the Cohort Program requirements” and “Held a minimum of one (1) in-person event attended by at least 50% of Cohort participants and mentors.” And, each participant must have: “90 minutes of educational programming through ≥3 sessions” and “180 minutes of one-on-one mentoring through ≥3 sessions.”
Important — how the 10 is counted: the required ten (10) completers must be the SAME individuals who each finished both the educational requirement (≥3 sessions / 90 min) and the mentoring requirement (≥3 sessions / 180 min). It is the intersection, not the sum — a cohort with 12 edu-done and 4 mentor-done has only as many completers as did both. The "Completers" column below is that intersection; the named individuals are listed underneath.
