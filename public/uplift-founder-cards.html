Uplift Founder Cards — Foundation Series · Summer 2026

Thirteen collectible trading cards (standard 2.5″ × 3.5″) in the coaster design: navy Looking Back front, white Looking Forward back with the pink ring — a title card, ten themed cards, The Founder wild card, and a Mentor Edition dedication card listing every Uplift mentor. Sheets are ordered front/back for duplex printing, plus belly-band wraps on the last page.

  1. Print on heavy cardstock, double-sided, flip on long edge, at 100% scale (no “fit to page”).
  2. Cut along dashed guides; round the corners with a corner punch (⅛″ radius) for a real trading-card feel.
  3. For a print shop (or makeplayingcards.com): order poker-size 2.5″ × 3.5″ cards, 300gsm+, rounded corners — they’ll take these designs directly.
