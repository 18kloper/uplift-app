The context

What Uplift is, and what a session is

The program

TechUnited:NJ's founder mentorship program. Eight weeks, around 70 vetted New Jersey founders, each matched with a mentor.

The sessions

Three a week, 30 minutes, all virtual. 22 of them between September 11 and November 4, at 12:30 or 5:30 ET. Speakers join from anywhere, so only the time slot has to work.

The room

Everyone is welcome at every session and about 30 come live. Founders need three to complete the program.

The brief

What would you talk about?

30 minutes, virtual, with time for founder questions. Almost anything a founder can act on works: if they can use it on Monday, it fits. Repeats of anything below are welcome, the founders are entirely different this program.

About seven minutes. No deck required, and your existing bio is fine.
