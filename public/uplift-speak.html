The context

What Uplift is, and what a session is

The program

Uplift is TechUnited:NJ's founder mentorship program. Eight weeks, September 9 to November 6. Every founder in it is matched with a mentor for a series of one-on-one meetings. This fall the cohort is around 70 vetted New Jersey founders.

The sessions

Three times a week the cohort comes together for a 30 minute virtual session. 22 of them across the program, at 12:30 or 5:30 ET. Outside speakers carry them, which is where you come in.

The room

Every founder is welcome at every session, and attendance runs around 30 live depending on the topic. Founders need three sessions to complete the program, so the people in front of you chose to be there.

The brief

If a founder can use it on Monday, it fits

That is the whole test. Sessions run as a presentation, a fireside chat where we interview you, or a working session where founders bring their own work. You pick what suits your material, and we settle the shape of it together on a call before the day.

The process

What happens after you apply

  1. We review within one business day

    You hear back either way, to confirm or to ask a couple of quick questions first.

  2. You get your highest available date

    We work down your ranking against what is still open.

  3. A calendar hold lands for that date

    So the slot is protected on both sides while the details come together.

  4. We get on a short sync call

    We map out the shape of the session, whether that is a fireside, a straight presentation, or a working session, and what the conversation should actually cover.

  5. We build and promote the event

    Your bio and headshot go on the Luma page, we promote it to the cohort, and you get the run of show and the stream details before the day.
