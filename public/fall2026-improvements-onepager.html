Internal · Uplift Mentorship Program · Fall 2026

Everything we improved from summer to fall.

Built from the summer retrospective and a full test run: applications through certificates, live in three working test portals. One goal underneath all of it: less admin chaos, more surface area for relationships.

The headline change · Meeting structure

Discover → Act → Roadmap. Front-loaded, named, intentional.

Meeting 1 · Within 7 days of matchDiscoverContext and trust. Pick the problem worth the hours together.
→
Meeting 2 · Within 10 days of thatActTurn advice into a move: one decision made, one experiment shipped.
→
Meeting 3 · By October 23RoadmapZoom out. The 90-day plan that outlasts the program.

All three land inside about 30 days, so founders enter the back half oriented instead of still scheduling. Each meeting has a job, a bring list, and a leave-with, and founders must review and confirm the structure in their portal before Meeting 1. The arc feeds Uplift at OverdriveAI on purpose: walk in with a mission from your Roadmap.

Program structure

8 weeks, one sprint: Sept 9 to Nov 6.

Down from 10 drifting weeks. Rolling clocks start when the match does, so late matches no longer break deadlines, and the whole mentor arc lands early enough to leave the back half for depth.

In-person

One required moment: Uplift at OverdriveAI, Oct 27.

Replaces Midpoint Meetup + Summit. Founders get showcased at TechUnited's marquee event instead of a program-only room. The 50% in-person attendance tracking survives, measured against OverdriveAI check-ins.

Week 1

Onboarding is a guided flow, not a wall of forms.

Welcome, confirm (by Sept 9), pick one of 7 templated cohort-branded sessions, pass the 10/10 onboarding quiz, then Deep Work unlocks. Five guarantees defined in a sentence each. Ten stacked text boxes became numbered steps founders can actually follow.

The gate

The mentor reveal is earned, and it's automatic.

Mentor stays TBD until onboarding is attended (auto-verified from the Luma tag), the quiz is passed, and Week 1 Deep Work is written. We cross-reference that Deep Work to validate every match before it's revealed.

Applications → portal

The Typeform feeds the portal directly.

Both applications are live and collecting. Each founder's portal is personalized from their answers: a My Company at a Glance overview page behind their clickable company name, focus areas pre-loaded into goals, and disclosure + NJ-residency data captured for the grant at review time.

Weekly rhythm

A heartbeat we can actually read.

Pulse check simplified to 🟢 A-okay / 🟡 check in on me / 🔴 not going as planned. Win of the Week feeds the Tuesday update to all mentors and mentees. Action items are tap-to-check and save as founders go.

Deep Work

Every prompt in the portal is one brand: Deep Work.

Goals, weekly focus, reflections, pre-meeting prompts. Required in Week 1, autosaving everywhere, and read by us to tailor the program. The more they put in, the more they get out.

Carrots

We promise only what every completer gets.

Two promises, out loud: the certificate of completion (signed, numbered, in-portal) and the OverdriveAI showcase. Everything else (spotlight articles, Founder Cards, alumni perks, Wrapped) stays a surprise, never a guarantee.

Verification

Paperwork happens as-you-go, not as a pile-up.

Meetings submitted right after they happen (date, length, notes; AI note-taker or written). Submissions are AI vetted and human verified. Completion is a BreezeDoc signature from founder and mentor confirming the log is accurate, then the certificate issues.

Content & docs

Everything a founder touches got rebuilt for fall.

All 22 educational sessions loaded with Luma links (three rotating formats). New program requirements doc, portal user guide, and the Discover / Act / Roadmap one-pager, embedded in the portal where founders must confirm they've read it.

Proven, not planned: three working test portals were run end to end this week from real sample applications (Kennedy · Kennedy Inc, Hana · Marcel, MJ · BarBelle), covering application ingest, the Week 1 gate, quiz pass, mentor reveal, meeting submission, and reset. What's left to build sits in the fall dashboard: Typeform ingest with one-click accept, meeting clocks, the pulse grid, and the wins feed.
