Uplift Mentorship Program · Fall 2026

Program requirements.

Everything you need to complete to graduate, with due dates and how each one is tracked. Eight weeks, September 9 to November 6, paperwork through November 20.

1

Confirm your participation

By Sept 9

One click in your portal on Week 1. It tells us you're in and moving forward.

Tracked automatically when you accept in the portal.

2

Attend an onboarding session

Sept 9–11

Pick any of the sessions on Wednesday, Thursday, or Friday of Week 1. Attending is what unlocks your mentor match.

Verified from session attendance by the program team.

3

Respond to your mentor within 72 hours

Rolling · all program

The communication commitment you made in your application: reply to your mentor, your PM, and TechUnited within 72 hours (communicated vacations excepted).

Flagged by the program team if the pattern breaks.

4

Meeting 1 · Discover

Within 7 days of your match

Your first hour: context, trust, and picking the problem worth your hours together. Submit it in the portal right after it happens.

Counts when submitted and verified. The submission form asks for date, length, and notes.

5

Meeting 2 · Act

Within 10 days of Meeting 1

Turn Discover into a move: one decision made, one experiment shipped. Submit it after.

Counts when submitted and verified.

6

Meeting 3 · Roadmap

By October 23

Zoom out and agree on the plan that runs past graduation. This is the one hard calendar deadline on your meetings.

Counts when submitted and verified. Meeting more than 3 times? Keep submitting; extra sessions count toward your tier commitment.

7

3 educational sessions

1 by Oct 1 · all 3 by Nov 6

Twenty-two sessions run across the program (Mon 12:30, Tue 5:30, Fri 12:30, ET). Any three count. Miss one you wanted? A brief written reflection covers it.

Attendance verified from Luma and updated in your milestones every Tuesday.

8

Attend Uplift at OverdriveAI

Oct 27 · In-person · Newark

Your one required in-person moment, at TechUnited's marquee event. RSVP through your portal early; check in on the night.

Checked in at the event. Conflict on Oct 27? Tell us now: uplift@techunited.co.

9

Weekly pulse check

Once a week · 10 seconds

Green: A-okay. Yellow: okay, but a check-in would be nice. Red: things are not going as planned. It helps us catch problems early.

Logged in your portal each program week.

10

End report and exit survey

Unlocks Nov 2 · Due by Nov 20

Five minutes on what moved for you and your company, plus the exit survey that mirrors your application so we can show your before and after.

Both live in your portal on Week 8.

11

Sign your paperwork

By Nov 20

You verify by signature that your logged educational sessions and mentor meetings are accurate. Your mentor signs off too. Keep clean logs all program and it's a two-minute task.

Forms sent to your email through BreezeDoc during the completion window, Nov 7–20.

AI note-taker: have Granola (preferred), Fathom, or Otter set up before your first mentor meeting, and tell your mentor which one you're running. Opt out and you're responsible for your own written notes.

🎓 Then your certificate is issued.

Complete everything above and your signed, numbered certificate of completion lands in your portal's Certificate tab, ready for LinkedIn.
