Your mentor card

Shows "Mentor TBD" until you've attended an onboarding session and completed your Week 1 Deep Work (the five things and your goals). Once everything is wrapped, we cross-reference your Week 1 Deep Work to double-check and validate the match, and to flag anything that might need adjusting. Your match then appears automatically in Week 2, no action needed from you.

My Journey · Weeks 1–8

Week 1 · Welcome & Onboarding Sept 9–13

  • Confirm your participation and attend an onboarding session (multiple slots Wed–Fri)
  • Review your application snapshot and define the five things every founder leaves with, a sentence each (required)
  • Do your Week 1 Deep Work: your goals and focus areas (required); the weekly prompts are optional
  • Bonus: AI Demo Night on Sept 10. Free ticket with code UPLIFT

Week 2 · Meet Your Mentor · Discover Sept 14–20

  • Your match appears here. Respond within 72 hours, schedule within 7 days
  • Review "How should I structure my meetings?" and acknowledge it (required before your first meeting)
  • Hold Discover, then hit Submit your first meeting

Week 3 · Act Sept 21–27

Meeting 2 is due within 10 days of Discover. Turn advice into action, submit the meeting after.

Weeks 4–6 · Find Your Rhythm, Keep Building, Roadmap Sept 28–Oct 25

  • Educational sessions every week: first of your 3 due by Oct 1
  • Deep Work prompts and weekly focus each week
  • Roadmap (Meeting 3) by October 23, the one hard deadline

Week 7 · Uplift at OverdriveAI Oct 27 · Required · In-person

TechUnited's marquee event and your required in-person moment. RSVP early through the portal, set your intentions in the Week 7 prompts, check in on the night.

Week 8 · Final Stretch & Completion Nov 2–6

Close out your meetings and sessions. The end report and exit surveys unlock Nov 2. Paperwork (including your signature verifying your logged sessions and meetings) runs Nov 7–20, then your certificate lands in the 🎓 Certificate tab.

🧠 Deep Work

Every prompt box in the portal is Deep Work: your goals, weekly focus, reflections, and pre-meeting prompts. They autosave as you type, they're yours to revisit, and the more you put in, the more you get out. We read them to tailor the program and validate your mentor match.
