Crewasis

Sharon Joseph, Founder & CEO · Cohort 3 · Mentor: Joe Spivack
AI / ML — Enterprise SaaSSeedRaising $2.0M · $300K committed · $10M post-money cap
Decision-intelligence platform for enterprise consumer brands — positioned as "Jira for enterprise consumer brands." Crewasis ingests multimodal market signals (reviews, social, search, first-party data), statistically ranks opportunities with its proprietary HAZRA algorithm, and turns them into cited, auditable, executed decisions via its "Winston" context layer — closing the gap where listening and agentic-marketing tools stop at insight. B2B SaaS: $60K starting ACV, $250K+ multi-brand enterprise contracts, 70%+ gross margin, 6:1 LTV:CAC.

Revenue history: $122K (2024) → $265K (2025) → $624K forecast (2026).

Obligo (HHALI LLC)

Hamza Ali Zafar, Founder · Cohort 1 · Mentor: Rahul Mehendale
SaaS — AI Agent GovernancePre-SeedSeeking design partners, not capital
Runtime governance for AI agents — "audits AI agents the way auditors audit employees: in real time, not once a year." Obligo parses contracts, regulations, and internal policies into a control graph, then continuously validates what agents actually do against what the company signed — allowed, flagged, or blocked, with evidence attached. Deterministic verification (LLM proposes, engine verifies against an open control catalog) is the claimed moat; runs inside the customer's environment so contracts never leave their network. Positioned as the fourth question next to Vanta/Drata (audit), Wiz (exposure), and Okta (identity): "is what we signed being enforced right now?" Founder has eight years building regulated data platforms, including PwC financial-analytics pipelines.
Online / Product
hhali.com/obligo — product runs end-to-end today
Money needed
No round stated — asking for five founding design partners with real policies/environments, plus introductions
Traction
2–3 live conversations with prospective design partners and investors; working end-to-end product built solo.
