Applications · the new front door

Both Typeforms are live and responses are arriving now. The dashboard pulls them in so review happens here instead of in the Typeform admin or a CSV.

New

What each application card shows

  • Mentees: company snapshot (the 12 traction questions), stage, industry, county, focus areas, session tier, 72-hour commitment, Oct 27 availability
  • Mentors: expertise tags, session tier commitment, availability, why they're mentoring
  • Disclosure chip on every card, straight from the application: Woman founder, Minority founder, both, or Undisclosed. This is the grant data, visible at review time instead of reconstructed later
  • New Jersey chip with the county. NJ residency is a hard requirement: the Accept button is disabled for out-of-state applications, so the only actions are decline or waitlist
  • One action per card: Accept creates the mentee/mentor record and drops them straight into the Matching queue. No CSV, no copy-paste

Matching · fed by the funnel
Changed

Accepting a match starts the program clock

Summer matching happened before the tooling existed, so late matches broke the week-based deadlines and needed hand-patched slug lists. Fall deadlines are relative to the match date, so a founder matched late still gets a fair, automatic schedule.

  • Queue shows accepted mentees with focus tags next to the mentor pool with expertise tags
  • Confirming a pair stamps matchDate. Meeting 1 (Discover) is due 7 days later, Meeting 2 (Act) 10 days after Meeting 1, Meeting 3 (Roadmap) by Oct 23 for everyone
  • Match Status tracks the confirmation email chain, with Need-to-Send and Non-Responsive as filters
On Track · the fall rulebook

Same three statuses the team already reads at a glance. Every trigger now mirrors the fall program plan instead of summer week numbers.

StatusFall triggers

Needs Attention Meeting 1 not logged by day 7 after match · Meeting 2 not logged 10 days after Meeting 1 · 2 pulse checks missed in a row · no educational session by Oct 1 · no Overdrive RSVP by Oct 13 · low-mood pulse streak (2+ weeks).
At Risk No mentor meeting 14 days after match (removal conversation) · Meeting 3 not logged after the Oct 23 hard deadline · unresponsive past the 72-hour SLA on two consecutive nudges.
