Logging In

Your portal URL is unique to you — it was emailed to you when you joined Uplift. The URL looks like uplift2026.vercel.app/your-first-name-here. Bookmark it — you'll be coming back to it each week throughout the program.

When you visit your link for the first time (or in a new browser), you'll be asked to enter your access code. This code is also in your welcome email from TechUnited:NJ. It's the first part of your URL — your first name. It's case-insensitive, so alex and Alex both work. If you can't find it, email uplift@techunited.co and we'll sort it out quickly.

uplift.
Uplift Summer 2026
Enter your access code to continue
Your session stays open. Once you enter your access code, your browser remembers you for the rest of the session. If you close the tab and come back, you may be asked to enter it again — this is normal. Your written responses are always saved, regardless of login state.

Here's what each element in the header does:

  • Your name and cohort — top left. Your cohort name (Edison, Hopper, Bardeen, Lawrence, or Morrison) is assigned based on your application focus area.

Unlike mentor meetings (which you submit yourself via Typeform), educational session attendance is verified manually by our team. We cross-reference Luma registration and attendance records. My Goals & Reflections

The My Goals & Reflections tab is your running journal for the entire program. Every reflection you write in My Journey — across all 9 weeks — is collected here in one place. At the top you'll see your primary and secondary goals from your application, followed by everything you've written week by week.

Think of it as a record of your thinking as it evolves. At the end of the program, you'll be able to look back at what you wrote in Week 1 and compare it to where you are now. Many founders find this the most valuable part of the portal.

Week 1 Reflections — Pre-Meeting Prep
Who is your ideal first customer, and why?
Mid-size marketing agencies with 10–50 employees. They have budget, they're tech-forward, and they feel the pain of manual reporting every month. They'll pay if I can save them 5 hours a week. The decision-maker is usually a Director of Operations, not the CEO — so I need to sell on efficiency, not vision.
What assumptions about your market have you not yet tested?
I'm assuming they'll pay $499/month. I haven't tested that. I also haven't validated whether they want AI-generated summaries or just clean exports — those are very different products. Need to run 5 discovery calls before building anything else.
Week 2 — Before Your First Mentor Meeting
What would make this first meeting feel like a success to you?
Walking away with a clear next experiment to run. Even just one. I don't need a roadmap — I just need to feel like I'm testing the right thing.
Your reflections are private to you and the Uplift team. They're not shared with your mentor, your cohort, or anyone outside the program. Write honestly — the more specific you are, the more useful these become when you look back at them.
