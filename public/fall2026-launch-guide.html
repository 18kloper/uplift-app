✕

Week-locked meeting deadlines

"Meet by week 2" broke every time a match ran late. Rolling deadlines (7 days from match, 10 days from Meeting 1) flex automatically, so the late-match patch lists in the code go away.

✕

Two buffer weeks of programming

The summer's catch-up weeks (6 and 9) mostly signaled that drifting was fine. Eight weeks with rolling deadlines keeps everyone closer to on-pace.
