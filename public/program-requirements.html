Uplift Summer 2026
Program Requirements
Everything you need to complete to graduate from Uplift, earn your certificate, and get the most out of this program.
📅 Program Dates: June 1 – August 4, 2026
There are 8 requirements to complete the Uplift Summer 2026 program and receive your Certificate of Completion. Most happen naturally as you engage with the program — attend your sessions, meet with your mentor, and show up for the key events. This document outlines each one, when it's due, and how it's tracked.
The 8 Requirements
1
Confirm Your Participation By Jun 3
Accept your spot in the program through your personal portal. This confirms your commitment and unlocks the rest of the program for you.
Tracked automatically when you confirm in your portal
2
Attend an Onboarding Session By Jun 7
Attend one of the five onboarding sessions held during Week 1 (Jun 1–6). Sessions are organized by cohort but you can attend any one that fits your schedule — they all cover the same material. This is also how your mentor match is confirmed.
Note: onboarding sessions do not count toward your 3 required educational sessions.
Confirmed manually by the Uplift team, updated every Tuesday
3
Attend 3 Educational Sessions By Aug 4
Attend at least 3 virtual educational sessions across the 9-week program. Sessions are offered every week in a variety of formats — Expert Insights, Industry Q&As, and Peer Development. All sessions are open to every mentee, regardless of cohort label. Register through Luma and show up.
Tip: don't wait until the end — sessions are spread across every week. Attending one every few weeks keeps you on track easily.
Verified manually by the Uplift team, updated every Tuesday
4
Complete 3 Mentorship Sessions Session 1 by Jun 13 · Session 2 by Jul 4 · Session 3 by Jul 18
Meet with your assigned mentor for a minimum of 3 sessions of at least 1 hour each (3 hours total). Each session must be recorded using Granola (the required AI note-taker, free at granola.ai) and submitted through your portal with your key takeaways.
Sessions submitted with a Granola transcript that are 60+ minutes are auto-verified instantly. Sessions without a transcript are reviewed manually by the team.
Submit each session through the Logged Mentorship Sessions tab in your portal
5
Attend the Midpoint Meetup Jun 23
Attend the in-person Midpoint Meetup on Tuesday, June 23rd, 4:00–7:00pm at Antique Lofts in Hoboken, NJ (2 min walk from the PATH). This is a required in-person event — register through Luma in advance.
Confirmed manually by the Uplift team after the event
6
Complete the End of Program Survey By Jul 25
Complete the 5-minute End of Program Report during Week 8. This is your final reflection on the program — your growth, your goals, and your experience. The link becomes available in your portal during Week 7.
Tracked automatically when you submit the report
7
Attend the Summit & Graduation Aug 4
Attend the Uplift Summit & Graduation on Tuesday, August 4th — an in-person celebration of everything you've built and accomplished this summer. You must attend to receive your Certificate of Completion. Register through Luma.
Confirmed manually by the Uplift team at the event
8
Receive Your Certificate of Completion After Aug 4
Once all 7 requirements above are verified, your Certificate of Completion is issued. It will appear in your portal under the Certificate sub-tab in My Journey. Certificates are processed and distributed following the Summit.
Issued by the Uplift team after all requirements are confirmed
