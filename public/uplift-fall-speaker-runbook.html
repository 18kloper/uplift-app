The thing being booked

What Uplift is, and what a session is

The program

Uplift is TechUnited:NJ's founder mentorship program. Eight weeks, Sept 9 to Nov 6. Every founder in it is matched with a mentor for three one-on-one meetings, and this fall the cohort is around 70 vetted New Jersey founders.

The sessions

Three times a week the cohort comes together for a 30 minute session, all virtual. 22 of them between Sept 11 and Nov 4, at 12:30 or 5:30. Outside speakers carry them, which is what these applications are for.

The room

Every founder is welcome at every session, and attendance runs around 30 live depending on the topic. Three sessions are required to complete the program, so the people who show up have a reason to be there.

The brief

If a founder can use it on Monday, it fits

That is the whole test. Formats rotate between a presentation, a fireside chat, and a working session, chosen by what the speaker's material actually needs.

Repeats are welcome. This is an entirely new group of founders, so a subject a past speaker covered is not taken. Same topic, new room, people who have never heard it.

The loop

What happens, in order, on both sides

The speaker's side runs on the left. Yours and the system's runs on the right. Everything converges on one decision, and that decision is the only thing that moves a slot.

You and the system

The decision

The only action that moves a slot. Everything here is reversible.

✓ Approve & book

The dropdown has already picked their highest-ranked date that is still open, with a star beside every date they asked for. One click books it.

✗ Decline

Recorded against their application, no slot involved. They stay in the Declined filter rather than disappearing.

undo

Puts them back to undecided and releases the slot. It appends a new row rather than erasing the old one, so the history survives.

The division of labor

What it does, and what stays yours

The rule I built to: everything reversible is automated, and everything that goes out with your name on it waits for you.

Handled for you

  • →Reading applications live out of Typeform, headshots and decks included
  • →Turning their date picks into real slots, with "any date works" opening all 22
  • →Pre-selecting the slot to book so the common case is one click
  • →Writing the decision, booking the slot, releasing it on undo
  • →Counting who is still waiting on you, on the Today list
  • →Flagging anyone who declined recording consent, before you book them

Still on you

  • →The yes or no itself. The board informs the call, it does not make it
  • →The email to the speaker. Nothing sends on your behalf
  • →Naming the Luma event and putting their bio and headshot on it
  • →Promoting the session to the cohort and your network
  • →The stream, the clips, and getting the clips back to them

Guardrails

The four ways it refuses to lose your work

No double bookings

Approving someone into a taken slot is refused by the server, naming who already has it. The guard lives in the API, so it holds even with two tabs open.

No silent save failures

If a decision does not reach the sheet, a red banner says so in plain words and tells you nothing was recorded, rather than showing a green badge that lies.

No fake blank slates

If the sheet is slow to answer, you get a warning that bookings may read as undecided, with an explicit "do not re-decide anyone off this view."

No slot needed to say no

A decline never touches the board, and an approve without a slot is rejected outright, so a booking can never exist without a date attached.

Known rough edges

Two things to keep in mind

01

The list does not poll on its own

It loads when you open the tab and refreshes itself after every decision. An application that arrives while you sit on the page shows up on reload. The Refresh button at the top still only refreshes the founder dashboard. One line of code to wire up if it annoys you.

02

Session 1 is the tight one

Fri Sept 11 is the first slot, and speakers need lead time to prepare and to promote it to their own audience. The early dates are the ones worth pushing hardest in the first week the link is out.

To go live

The order that keeps your one-day promise honest

The form is already taking submissions and every one is stored safely in Typeform. Distribution starts with the mentors who already flagged interest, then widens.

  1. Deploy the admin

    Commit and push, and Vercel picks it up. Until then the Speakers tab only exists on your machine.

  2. Email the mentors who already said yes

    The rolling list of mentors who flagged interest on their application goes first. They are the warmest audience you have and they are already in.

  3. Then send the team the forwarding ask

    Teammates drop the blurb into the group chats they are already in. That is the cheapest reach you have, and it only needs to fill what the mentors do not.

  4. Check the board once a day

    The Today list tells you when someone is waiting. Clear it same day and the one business day promise holds by itself.

  5. Name the Luma events as they book

    A named event with a real speaker on it registers founders. A shell does not.
