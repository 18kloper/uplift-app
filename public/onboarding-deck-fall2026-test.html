Set your expectations right

How your mentor is meant to support you.

There are a million ways a stranger with more experience can help you. That’s the whole point.

What they might do

  • Open up their network
  • Share advice, and the anecdotes behind it
  • Tell you what worked, and what didn’t, for them
  • Nudge you toward a resource or direction worth trying, and just as often, one that isn’t
  • Just listen, as a sounding board

What mentorship isn’t

  • Building your product, or doing the work for you
  • A consultant, or a coach paid to always cheer you on
  • Someone who’s lived your exact scenario in your exact industry, their edge is usually adjacent, and that still counts
  • Guaranteed answers on demand
  • A job. Mentors volunteer their time, same as you’d hope someone would for you

Your match, demystified

What your mentor will know about you.

Your mentor gets your company at a glance before your first meeting, pulled straight from your application. Your cohort-mates can see it too, in the Cohort Directory. One exception: your revenue dollar range stays between you and your mentor.

How your match is actually made

A matching tool built for this program scores every founder–mentor pair on five factors, then a person on our team confirms every match. Nothing is auto-assigned.

  • Time-commitment fit: your cadence vs what they signed up to give
  • Focus-area alignment: what you need vs what they know best
  • Your own words: what you asked for vs what they offer
  • Stage fit
  • Schedule overlap

Full write-up lives in your Resources tab: How We Match Founders & Mentors. So the answer to “why this mentor?” is never a shrug.

Set Yourself Up for Success

How to structure your one-on-one meetings: Discover, Act, Roadmap.

Whatever session count you signed up for, here’s the suggested arc. Each meeting does its own job.

MEETING 1 · WITHIN 7 DAYS OF MATCH
Discover
THE JOB: Context and trust. Pick the problem worth your hours together.

BRING

  • Your goals reflection from the portal
  • Where the company actually is: traction, runway, blockers
  • The one decision you keep circling

LEAVE WITH

  • One focus area you both agree matters most
  • What winning looks like by November
  • Your next meeting on the calendar
MEETING 2 · WITHIN 10 DAYS OF MEETING 1
Act
THE JOB: Turn Discover into a move. Advice becomes action here.

BRING

  • What you tried since your previous meeting, including what flopped
  • Real numbers, real customer conversations
  • The obstacle that surprised you

LEAVE WITH

  • One concrete commitment with a date on it
  • An experiment, a decision, or an intro your mentor can make
  • What to measure before your next meeting
MEETING 3 · SUGGESTED BY OCT 23
Roadmap
THE JOB: Zoom out. Agree on the long-term plan and how you’ll keep meeting.

BRING

  • Results from your Act commitment
  • An honest read on what’s changed since your Discover meeting
  • Your biggest open question for the next quarter

LEAVE WITH

  • Your next three moves, in order
  • The signals that tell you it’s working
  • Whether you’re meeting again, and how often
