What's to come

Over the next couple of weeks we're actively matching you with your mentor. No coin flips and no alphabetical order. Your match is built from two things:

One
The application you submitted. Where your company is today, what you want help with, and how much time you have.
Two
Your Week 1 action items. What you tell us during onboarding week sharpens the match before we finalize it.

Session commitment carries the most weight, then focus overlap, stage, schedule, and the language you and your mentor both used. Every pairing is confirmed by a person, never assigned by a machine.

So when do I actually get my mentor?

Two things have to happen, in this order.

1. You finish all of onboarding. Attend an onboarding session, pass the short onboarding quiz, and answer your Week 1 Deep Work.
2. We cross-reference our initial match against your Week 1 Deep Work. Your answers there are the most current thing we have about where you are and what you need, so we hold the pairing up against them before we confirm it.

Once both are done, your mentor appears in your portal. No email to wait on, and no chasing us.
