Completion criteria: each mentee must complete a minimum of 3 mentor sessions (180 minutes) of 1:1 mentorship. Verification (two layers): (1) mentees log each session through the mentor-meeting intake form (Typeform) recording date, duration, and takeaways — credited minutes unlock milestones at 60 / 120 / 180; (2) each mentor then certifies completion by e-signing a Mentor Verification Form (BreezeDoc) affirming “meetings greater than three (3), totaling greater than 180 minutes,” with the session dates listed. A mentee is verified complete when the mentor-signed form is on file. Forms for the remaining mentors were issued August 3–4 and are tracked below.
Cohort assignments: tables reflect the current cohort composition of record (updated August 2026); several participants were re-assigned across cohorts since the July 27 report.
