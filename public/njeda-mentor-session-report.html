Completion criteria: each mentee must complete a minimum of 3 mentor sessions (180 credited minutes) of 1:1 mentorship. Verification: sessions are logged by mentees through the mentor-meeting intake form (Typeform), each recording the session date, duration, notes, and key takeaways; credit is calculated by actual minutes logged (every 60 minutes = one session-equivalent, so milestones unlock at 60 / 120 / 180 min). Submissions are individually reviewed; duplicate or undocumented entries are excluded. Mentor assignments reflect the current portal matches. Goal: ≥10 mentees complete per cohort. Each cohort header shows those complete, those complete or in progress (≥1 session), and the deficit still needed to reach 10. Mentees ranked by credited minutes; those with no logged session are grayed.
