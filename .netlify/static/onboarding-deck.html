What success looks like

Program expectations
over 10 weeks.

Required commitments

  • 1.5 hours of educational programming (virtual, 30-min sessions, all registered through Luma)
  • 3 hours of 1:1 mentor time
  • Keep your portal up to date with what you’ve completed
  • Log every mentor meeting via the form in your portal
  • Regularly check your email for weekly updates, mentor outreach, and program info

In-person moments

  • June 23, 4 to 7pm, Hoboken (midpoint)
  • August 4, 4 to 7pm, Hoboken (Summit)

Miss a session? A brief written reflection counts.

A quick housekeeping note

Log your mentorship
meetings as you go.

We verify mentor hours at the end of the program, so an easy log keeps things smooth.

Why we ask

  • Helps confirm your 3 hours of 1:1 mentor time
  • Gives your mentor what they need to sign off at program end
  • Captures takeaways so progress is easy to see
REQUIRED TOOL
Granola

A notetaker and transcriber that runs alongside your calls. It captures the meeting so you don’t have to take notes manually, and it gives you a clean record we can use to verify your sessions.

Everyone should download it before your first mentor meeting.
